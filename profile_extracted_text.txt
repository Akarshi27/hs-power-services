=== PAGE 12 ===
E.V. CHARGING  STATION
H.S. Power Services is a dedicated business platform focused on enabling reliable
infrastructure for electric mobility by providing 30 kVA to 60 kVA fast-charging EV
stations. The company aims to bridge the gap in accessible, high-capacity charging
solutions, supporting the rapid adoption of electric vehicles across urban and semi-
urban regions. In collaboration with major industry players such as Adani and
WeGreen, along with NRN, H.S. Power Services is working toward building a
sustainable and scalable EV ecosystem. Their solutions emphasize efficiency, reduced
charging time, and long-term environmental benefits, contributing to India’s
transition toward clean energy and green transportation.
