About H.S. Power Services

A registered partnership firm established in 2017, providing comprehensive contractual solutions across telecom infrastructure, commercial solar energy, EV fast charging stations, and civil engineering.

Established Technical Expertise & Operational Discipline

H.S. Power Services is a partnership firm headquartered in Jankipuram Extension, Lucknow. We operate as a dedicated contractual service provider to leading telecom tower infrastructure providers, solar power developers, EV charging network operators, and civil construction companies.

Our service model is grounded in qualified engineering supervision, end-to-end site execution from civil casting to power commissioning, and structured preventive maintenance to ensure high network uptime.

Our Mission

To provide best engineering services at lowest cost while maintaining rigorous technical standards.

Our Vision

To ensure total client satisfaction through dependable execution and zero-punch-point site delivery.

Our Goals

To expand our contractual capabilities from UP and MP across all of India.
