Company Profile

Engineering Integrity. Disciplined Field Execution.

H.S. Power Services is a registered partnership firm established in 2017 with its head office located in Jankipuram Extension, Lucknow. Founded by partners Sanjeev Srivastava and H.K. Singh, we operate as an end-to-end contractual engineering and infrastructure services provider supporting telecommunications operators, renewable energy developers, electric mobility providers, and heavy civil contractors.

Our field teams focus on preventive maintenance and precision site installation to minimize unanticipated production interruptions, major breakdowns, and punch points. Backed by qualified project engineers, we manage projects from initial site acquisition to final technical commissioning.
