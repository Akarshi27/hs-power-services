Engineering Capabilities & Scope of Work

Detailed technical breakdown of our four documented core infrastructure areas, backed by verified field experience, qualified project engineers, and disciplined execution.

Discipline 01

Telecom & Network Infrastructure

H.S. Power Services provides comprehensive cell tower installation, plant manufacturing, and conditional monitoring across UP East. Our operations focus heavily on preventive maintenance to minimize the possibility of unanticipated production interruption or major breakdowns by uncovering structural or electrical issues before failure occurs. Other services include scheduled breakdown and predictive maintenance services.

In small cell sites across UP East and Madhya Pradesh (MP), our services begin with site acquisition focused on minimizing disputes with landowners, followed by site installation with guaranteed zero punch points.

Documented Scope of Work:

✓ PIU Installation (Power Interface Unit)
✓ Battery Bank Installation & Load Testing
✓ Power Plant Installation & Commissioning
✓ DG Installation, Locking & Battery Caging
✓ Tower Foundation Construction & Rebar
✓ Electrical Work & Upgradation
✓ Small Cell Site Acquisition (Dispute-Free)
✓ Pole (9mtr / 6mtr) Installation
✓ Electricity Board (EB) Power Connections
✓ RF Cabling & Scheduled Breakdown Support

Discipline 02

Renewable Energy (Commercial & Industrial Solar)

H.S. Power Services is a dedicated business platform that provides comprehensive turnkey solutions combining development, financing, construction, and operation of solar rooftops for commercial and industrial applications.

In addition, we provide ongoing Operations & Maintenance (O&M) to ensure extended lifetime value and project durability. We manage technical feasibility checks, structural load analysis, net metering, and compliance with UP NEDA regulations.

Solar Scope & Deliverables:

✓ Turnkey Commercial & Industrial Solar Rooftops
✓ High-Tensile Galvanized Mounting Structures
✓ Inverter Station & ACDB/DCDB Interconnection
✓ Net Metering & Electricity Board Liaisoning
✓ UP NEDA Guidelines & Approvals Compliance
✓ Preventative & Corrective Solar O&M Support

Discipline 03

30 kVA to 60 kVA Fast EV Charging Stations

HSPS enables reliable infrastructure for electric mobility by providing 30 kVA to 60 kVA fast-charging EV stations across urban and semi-urban transit corridors. Working toward scalable charging ecosystems in collaboration with major industry players such as Adani, WeGreen, NRN, MLL Mobility, and Rozana Rural Commerce.

We execute complete turnkey station development: reinforced concrete plinths, weatherproof canopies, transformer interfacing, dual-gun fast DC pedestal mounting, dedicated LT distribution panels, and chemical earthing pits.

EV Charging Specifications:

✓ 30 kVA & 60 kVA Fast DC Charging Pedestals
✓ Dual-Gun CCS2 / Type 2 Configurations
✓ Heavy Civil Foundation Plinths & Canopies
✓ Transformer Step-Down & LT Substation Works
✓ Commercial Fleet & Semi-Urban Hub Setup
✓ 24/7 Breakdown Response & Preventive Testing
