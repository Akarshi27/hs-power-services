# H.S. Power Services - Official Corporate Website

[![Deploy to Render](https://render.com/images/deploy-to-render-button.svg)](https://render.com/deploy?repo=https://github.com/Akarshi27/hs-power-services)

Corporate web platform built for **H.S. Power Services (HSPS)**, a premier contractual engineering and infrastructure provider based in Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh.

---

## ⚡ Core Business Verticals Represented

1. **Telecom Cell Towers & Network Infrastructure (40% Share)**
   - Macro Cell Tower Installation, Structural Erection & Foundation Casting
   - Power Interface Unit (PIU) & DC Power Plant Commissioning
   - Battery Bank Installation, Testing, and Battery Caging
   - DG Installation, Automated AMF Integration & Anti-Theft DG Locking
   - 5G Network Rollouts & Jio Fiber / Cable Upgradation
   - Routine Preventive Maintenance & 24/7 Breakdown Response across UP East

2. **Renewable Energy & Solar Rooftops (30% Share)**
   - Turnkey EPC for Commercial & Industrial (C&I) Rooftop Solar Plants
   - UP NEDA Liaisoning, Net Metering & Grid Interconnection
   - Structural Solar Mounting Engineering & Inverter Setup
   - Comprehensive Operations & Maintenance (O&M) for Lifetime Yield Durability

3. **Fast EV Charging Stations (15% Share)**
   - 30 kVA to 60 kVA Fast DC Dual-Gun Charging Hubs
   - Strategic Collaborations with Adani, WeGreen, NRN & Rozana Rural Commerce
   - Civil Plinth, Transformer Coordination, Substation & Heavy Power Cabling

4. **Small Cell Sites & Municipal Smart Poles (15% Share)**
   - High-Density Urban Telecom Densification across UP East and Madhya Pradesh (MP)
   - Site Acquisition with Dispute-Free Landowner Liaisoning
   - 6-Meter & 9-Meter Smart Municipal Pole Erection
   - Electricity Board (EB) Connection Liaisoning & RF Cabling

5. **Heavy Civil Infrastructure & Railway OHE Electrification**
   - Railway Overhead Electrification (OHE) Mast Foundations (KEC International & TBEA India)
   - Heavy Building & Industrial Civil Construction (NCC Limited)

## 🚀 How to Run & Preview Locally

A local HTTP server is active on your machine:
- Open your browser and navigate to: **`http://localhost:8888/index.html`**

Or manually start the server at any time with PowerShell / Command Prompt:
```powershell
cd "c:\Users\akars\OneDrive\Desktop\HS Power Services"
python -m http.server 8888
```

You can also simply double-click **`index.html`** to open it directly in any modern web browser (Edge, Chrome, Brave, Firefox).

## 🌐 Free Deployment Options

- **GitHub Pages:** Create a repository on GitHub, upload this folder, and enable GitHub Pages under repository settings.
- **Netlify / Vercel:** Simply drag and drop the `HS Power Services` folder onto Netlify Drop ([app.netlify.com/drop](https://app.netlify.com/drop)) for instantaneous custom URL hosting with free SSL.
- **cPanel / Domain Hosting:** Upload the contents into your `public_html` directory via FTP or cPanel File Manager.
